Creative Designer

Experience: 1–2 years
Job Type: Freelance / Full-time
Location: Delhi NCR

About the Role

We are looking for a Creative Designer with a strong visual sense and an interest in music, entertainment, sports, fashion and pop culture. The role involves working on brand campaigns, digital content, creative concepts and visual communication.

Key Responsibilities

    Design creative assets for brand campaigns, social media and digital platforms.
    Develop visual concepts, layouts and moodboards for campaigns and projects.
    Translate creative briefs and ideas into compelling visual outputs.

What We're Looking For

    1–2 years of experience in graphic or visual design, with a strong portfolio.
    Proficiency in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign; working knowledge of Figma.
    Strong understanding of typography, layouts, composition and visual storytelling.
    Hands-on experience with AI-powered creative tools and workflows, including AI-assisted image generation. Familiarity with tools like Midjourney, Higgsfield, Claude or similar AI tools is a plus.
    Creative, detail-oriented and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment.

Engagement: Open to both full-time and freelance opportunities.

Pay: ₹15,000.00 - ₹30,000.00 per month

Work Location: Hybrid remote in Delhi, Delhi
